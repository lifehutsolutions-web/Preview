Our Story

From Indian soil to your table, honestly grown.

Founded in 2017 by chef Ananya Rao, Verdant Table began as a single stall at a Bengaluru farmers' market. Today, it's a full dining destination built on one promise: every ingredient is organic, seasonal, and traceable to the farmer who grew it.

We work directly with over 40 smallholder farms across Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, cutting out middlemen so farmers earn fairly and our kitchen gets produce that's often harvested the same morning it's served.

  • 🌱 100% chemical-free, certified organic sourcing
  • ♻️ Zero-waste kitchen, compost returned to partner farms
  • 🤝 Fair-trade pricing directly with 40+ Indian farms
  • 👩‍🍳 Menus rewritten weekly around what's in season
