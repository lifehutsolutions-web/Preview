Page One

The story behind every fold

VARQ opened its first page in 2019 in a Mumbai bylane, born from one question: why should a multi-cuisine menu feel scattered? Our kitchen team — Indian, Pan-Asian and European trained — writes every dish as part of one connected story, plated on silver vark the way celebration food has always been finished across India.

Today VARQ runs live tandoor and sushi counters in three Indian cities, sourcing produce from regional farms and finishing each plate the way you'd finish a well-loved page — with care, not clutter.
