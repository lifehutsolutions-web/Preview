The Cutting Table

Five steps, one seam at a time

From first measurement to final fitting, here's the exact order your garment travels through our atelier.

  1. 01

    Measure

    27-point measurement session, in-studio or at your home.

  2. 02

    Design

    Choose fabric, lapel, lining and finish with your tailor.

  3. 03

    Cut

    A paper pattern is drafted and cut solely for your frame.

  4. 04

    Stitch

    Hand and machine finishing across 30-40 hours of work.

  5. 05

    Fit

    Two follow-up fittings ensure it moves the way you do.
