Curator's Note

A studio that treats every brief like a body of work.

Founded in Jaipur in 2018, Studio Antara pairs the discipline of Indian craft traditions with the clarity of contemporary digital design. We keep a small roster of clients each year so that every project — a fintech app, a textile label, a metro wayfinding system — gets the same close attention a conservator gives a canvas.

Our process is slow where it needs to be, and fast where it counts. No templates lifted from a stock library, no identity kits copied twice. Just work that earns its place on the wall.

Wing III — Conservation Room

Behind the canvas.

How a piece moves from brief to wall — the same four stages, every time.

  1. Stage I

    Acquisition

    A working session to understand the brief, audience and constraints — no proposal goes out until we've asked the uncomfortable questions.

  2. Stage II

    Sketching

    Rough studies — moodboards, wireframes or type trials — shared early, so direction is agreed before detail work begins.

  3. Stage III

    Framing

    The chosen direction is built out in full: systems, screens, or physical signage, refined across two structured review rounds.

  4. Stage IV

    Hanging

    Launch, handover and a documented style guide — plus thirty days of light-touch support once the piece is on the wall.
