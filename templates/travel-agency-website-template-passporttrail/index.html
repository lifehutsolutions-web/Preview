A Peek Into Your Trip

7 Days · Kerala Backwaters & Hills

Every day of your journey, laid out like a segment on your ticket. Tap a day to unfold it.

DEP: Your City 21:00  →  ARR: Kochi 08:00

Board your PassportTrail coach and arrive at Kochi. Check in, freshen up, then explore Chinese fishing nets, Fort Kochi's colonial streets, and Mattancherry Palace before an evening Kathakali performance.

DEP: Kochi 07:00  →  ARR: Munnar 11:30

Drive through spice plantations and waterfalls to Munnar. Visit tea gardens, the Tea Museum, and catch sunset over rolling hills from Top Station viewpoint.

DEP: Munnar 08:00  →  ARR: Thekkady 10:30

A boat safari through Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, spice garden tour, and an evening at leisure by the lake.

DEP: Thekkady 09:00  →  ARR: Alleppey 12:30

Two days cruising the backwaters aboard a traditional Kettuvallam houseboat — home-cooked Kerala meals, village visits and a night moored under the stars.

DEP: Alleppey 09:00  →  ARR: Kovalam 12:00

Relax on Kovalam's lighthouse beach, optional ayurvedic massage, then board the overnight coach back home — a fresh stamp in hand.
